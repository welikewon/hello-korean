Living in Korea · 한국 생활

Hello, Korean! · 안녕, 한국어!

No Korean voice on this phone yet. iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Korean. Android: Settings → text-to-speech → install Korean voice data. Or tap “Google Translate” under a line.
🙈 Hide Korean hides every line: play it, say what it means, then tap the line to check. 🔁 Repeat plays one line again and again (tap again to stop).
