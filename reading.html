A day with a Korean friend · 한국 친구와 함께한 하루

오늘 한국 친구를 만났어요.
o-neul han-guk chin-gu-reul man-na-sseo-yo
Today I met a Korean friend.
Google Translate
우리는 같이 한국 식당에 갔어요.
u-ri-neun ga-chi han-guk sik-dang-e ga-sseo-yo
We went to a Korean restaurant together.
Google Translate
저는 불고기를 먹고, 친구는 떡볶이를 먹었어요.
jeo-neun bul-go-gi-reul meok-go, chin-gu-neun tteok-bo-kki-reul meo-geo-sseo-yo
I ate bulgogi, and my friend ate tteokbokki.
Google Translate
떡볶이는 조금 매웠어요.
tteok-bo-kki-neun jo-geum mae-wo-sseo-yo
The tteokbokki was a little spicy.
Google Translate
그래서 물을 많이 마셨어요.
geu-rae-seo mu-reul ma-ni ma-syeo-sseo-yo
So we drank a lot of water.
Google Translate
그다음에 카페에서 커피를 마셨어요.
geu-da-eu-me ka-pe-e-seo keo-pi-reul ma-syeo-sseo-yo
Then we had coffee at a café.
Google Translate
우리는 가위바위보도 했어요. 제가 이겼어요!
u-ri-neun ga-wi-ba-wi-bo-do hae-sseo-yo. je-ga i-gyeo-sseo-yo
We played rock-paper-scissors too. I won!
Google Translate
헤어질 때 “또 만나요!” 하고 인사했어요.
he-eo-jil ttae “tto man-na-yo!” ha-go in-sa-hae-sseo-yo
When we said goodbye, we said “See you again!”
Google Translate
집에서 친구를 위해 기도했어요.
ji-be-seo chin-gu-reul wi-hae gi-do-hae-sseo-yo
At home I prayed for my friend.
Google Translate
정말 행복한 하루였어요!
jeong-mal haeng-bo-kan ha-ru-yeo-sseo-yo
It was a really happy day!
Google Translate
